# 🌍 Amazonga — Global Multi-Marketplace Amazon Search Engine

**Live Demo:** [**https://ihosam.github.io/Amazonga/**](https://ihosam.github.io/Amazonga/)

**Amazonga** is a fast, client-side global search engine that lets you search, filter, and compare products across all **23 official Amazon versions worldwide** (`US`, `UK`, `JP`, `DE`, `SA`, `EG`, `FR`, `AE`, `IT`, `ES`, `CA`, `AU`, `IN`, `NL`, `SE`, `PL`, `TR`, `SG`, `MX`, `BR`, `BE`, `IE`, `ZA`) from a single interface.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- **🌐 23 Global Amazon Marketplaces**:
  - **Featured Core**: United States (`amazon.com`), United Kingdom (`amazon.co.uk`), Japan (`amazon.co.jp`), Germany (`amazon.de`), Saudi Arabia (`amazon.sa`), Egypt (`amazon.eg`), France (`amazon.fr`)
  - **Middle East & Africa**: Saudi Arabia (`SA`), UAE (`AE`), Egypt (`EG`), South Africa (`ZA`)
  - **Americas**: United States (`US`), Canada (`CA`), Mexico (`MX`), Brazil (`BR`)
  - **Europe**: UK (`UK`), Germany (`DE`), France (`FR`), Italy (`IT`), Spain (`ES`), Netherlands (`NL`), Sweden (`SE`), Poland (`PL`), Turkey (`TR`), Belgium (`BE`), Ireland (`IE`)
  - **Asia-Pacific**: Japan (`JP`), India (`IN`), Australia (`AU`), Singapore (`SG`)
- **⚡ Auto-Converted Local Currency Price Filters**:
  - Enter your Min/Max budget in your preferred base currency (`USD`, `SAR`, `EGP`, `AED`, `EUR`, `GBP`, `JPY`, etc.) and Amazonga automatically converts the price range into each country's local currency using live FX rates and applies Amazon's native `rh=p_36` price filter parameter.
- **🎯 Smart ASIN & Product Link Detector**:
  - Paste any 10-character Amazon **ASIN** (e.g., `B09XS7JWHH`) or any regional Amazon product URL, and Amazonga automatically switches to direct `/dp/{ASIN}` product lookup across every Amazon store worldwide, complete with **Keepa** and **CamelCamelCamel** price history shortcuts.
- **🗣️ Force English Interface (`language=en_*`)**:
  - Automatically appends the English language parameter to non-English Amazon marketplaces (`DE`, `JP`, `SA`, `EG`, `FR`, `ES`, `IT`, `NL`, `PL`, `SE`, `TR`) for effortless browsing.
- **🛰️ Google, DuckDuckGo & Bing Site X-Ray Search**:
  - Run `site:amazon.*` X-Ray searches for individual marketplaces or across multiple Amazon domains simultaneously.
- **⚖️ Cross-Border Price Calculator**:
  - Enter local prices from different Amazon stores to immediately see which country offers the lowest price in your home currency.
